- **Title:** Equality in Nikiforov's average-neighbour-degree bounds for $A_\alpha$
- **Authors:** Aleksei Kudriashov
- **Abstract** (1147 characters, limit 1920):

  For a graph without isolated vertices, Nikiforov bounded the spectral radius of $A_\alpha=\alpha D+(1-\alpha)A$ by the minimum and maximum of $\alpha d(v)+(1-\alpha)m(v)$, where $m(v)$ is the average degree of the neighbours of $v$. We study equality for connected, nonregular graphs and $0<\alpha<1$. Such a graph has at most one equality parameter; it is rational, belongs to $(0,1/2]$, and forces $A_\alpha$ to be singular. At $\alpha=1/2$ the graphs are precisely the nonregular semiregular bipartite graphs. We give an integral recognition criterion and a complete classification when exactly two degrees occur. Conversely, every rational number in $(0,1/2]$ occurs. For every $k\ge 2$ an explicit connected $2k$-vertex graph with $k$ distinct degrees attains equality at $\alpha=1/3$; thus no absolute bound on degree variety is possible. An exact isomorphism-class census through ten vertices, independently generated by nauty and checked with rational arithmetic, yields $599$ irregular cases at order ten. We distinguish proofs from finite computer certificates, and delimit the prior-art search rather than asserting publication novelty.
