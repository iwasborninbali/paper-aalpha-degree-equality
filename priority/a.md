# Independent priority audit A — 2026-11-15

**Scope.** Athanor laboratory (Aleksei Kudriashov), manuscript on equality in the connected-graph bounds (20)--(21) of V. Nikiforov, *Merging the A- and Q-spectral theories*, *Applicable Analysis and Discrete Mathematics* **11** (2017), 81--107, DOI [10.2298/AADM1701081N](https://doi.org/10.2298/AADM1701081N), Proposition 20, printed pp. 92--93, [full PDF](https://pefmath.etf.bg.ac.rs/vol11num1/AADM-Vol11-No1-81-107.pdf). This is an independent **publication-priority** investigation, not a certification of correctness or a claim of exhaustive discovery of all scholarly literature. Cutoff: 2026-11-15. No other priority auditor's report was inspected.

**Exact test.** For finite, simple, connected, nonregular G, write d(v)>0, s(v)=sum of degrees of its neighbors, m(v)=s(v)/d(v), and A_alpha=alpha D+(1-alpha)A. Equality in either min/max bound is equivalent, via irreducibility and Collatz--Wielandt, to the **same** eigenvector equation
\[
 A_\alpha d=\rho(A_\alpha)d,\qquad
 \alpha d(v)^2+(1-\alpha)s(v)=\rho(A_\alpha)d(v)\quad\text{for every }v.
\]
This prevents a serious bibliographic mistake: equality in Nikiforov's edge-based bounds (22)--(23), or in other A_alpha bounds, is **not** the same research question.

## Verdicts

### 1. Uniqueness, rationality, alpha <= 1/2, equality at 1/2, and two-degree equitable classification

**PARTLY PUBLISHED.** The alpha=1/2 clause is **already published**: L. Feng and G. Yu, *On three conjectures involving the signless Laplacian spectral radius of graphs*, *Publications de l'Institut Mathematique* **85(99)** (2009), 35--38, DOI [10.2298/PIM0999035F](https://doi.org/10.2298/PIM0999035F), **Lemma 2.3 on pp. 36--37**. It states that, for connected G, equality in q(G) <= max_v [d(v)+m(v)] holds precisely for regular or semiregular bipartite graphs and proves it via D^{-1}QD. Because A_(1/2)=Q/2, the claimed endpoint is exactly this result. Nikiforov (2017), **Proposition 20, p. 92**, established the alpha>1/2 connected equality iff regular and restated the Q-case; the remark on **p. 93** presents the other interior cases as apparently absent. S. Liu, K. C. Das and J. Shu, *On the eigenvalues of A_alpha-matrix of graphs*, *Discrete Mathematics* **343** (2020), article **111917**, DOI [10.1016/j.disc.2020.111917](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2020.111917), **Lemma 2.9**, again states the d+m characterization and cites earlier literature.

**No exact previous publication located** for the conjunction of (i) at most one possible alpha for each nonregular connected graph, (ii) rationality of that alpha, or (iii) the exact classification via equitable **two-degree** partitions. The elementary formulas themselves must not be advertised as impossible for others to have observed: the row-sum criterion is in Nikiforov and general Perron--Frobenius theory, and degree/neighbor-degree equations have a long literature. In particular H. Deng and H. Huang, *On the Main Signless Laplacian Eigenvalues of a Graph*, *Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra* **26** (2013), **381--393**, DOI [10.13001/1081-3810.1659](https://doi.org/10.13001/1081-3810.1659), **Theorem 2.6 on p. 385**, characterizes graphs with two main Q-eigenvalues by a different parabolic condition s(v)=-d(v)^2+a d(v)-b. It is related but does not establish the asserted interior-alpha uniqueness/equitability theorem. Equitable-partition matrix methods are older general machinery, not the requested specific classification.

### 2. All rational alpha in (0,1/2], split joins, and the Nikiforov remark

**PARTLY PUBLISHED (an implicit special case), exact full claims NOT FOUND.** A. Alhevaz et al., *On the A_alpha-spectral radius of connected graphs*, *Ars Mathematica Contemporanea* **23** (2023), article **P1.06**, DOI [10.26493/1855-3974.2697.43a](https://doi.org/10.26493/1855-3974.2697.43a), **Remark 2.4, pp. 5--6**, explicitly uses the nonregular graph K_4-e = K_2 join complement(K_2), computes its vertex degrees (2,3) and neighbor-average degrees (3,7/3), and writes the two expressions 3-alpha and 7/3+(2/3)alpha with a **breakpoint alpha=0.4**. At alpha=2/5 those expressions coincide. Combining this **published numerical calculation** with Nikiforov's already-published equality criterion gives the exact nonregular equality example at alpha=2/5. **Caveat:** Alhevaz et al. do not explicitly say they refute Nikiforov's remark, and a printed breakpoint is not the same thing as a stated equality theorem. Nonetheless priority for this *particular* witness must be qualified; do not present K_4-e at 2/5 as a wholly unprecedented observation.

**Not located in a prior source:** the full two-parameter construction K_t join complement(K_m) with alpha=m/(2m+t-1) as an equality theorem; the counterexample expressly framed against the p. 93 remark; or the result that **every** rational alpha in (0,1/2] occurs. The different construction consisting of a K_(r+1) with p leaves appended to each core vertex, r=q-2p and alpha=p/q, proves the claimed existence internally but was not found explicitly in the literature examined. General published A_alpha spectra of joins and split graphs are **not** by themselves a statement about equality in (20)--(21). Compare M. Basunia, P. Mahato, R. Kannan, *On A_alpha-Spectra of Some Join Graphs*, DOI [10.1007/s40840-021-01166-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40840-021-01166-z); and A. E. Brondani, F. A. M. Franca, C. S. Oliveira, *Positive semidefiniteness of A_alpha(G) on some families of graphs*, *Discrete Applied Mathematics* **323** (2022), 113--123, DOI [10.1016/j.dam.2020.12.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2020.12.007). These address spectra or positive-semidefiniteness, not this full equality-family result.

### 3. Connected 2k-vertex, k-degree equality examples at alpha=1/3 for every k>=2

**NOT FOUND** in the examined publications. The claimed staircase family has two vertices of degree i for every i=1,...,k and satisfies s(v)=d(v)(2k+1-d(v))/2, i.e. s=-d^2/2+((2k+1)/2)d; hence A_(1/3)d=((2k+1)/3)d. I found **related but inequivalent** unbounded-degree theorems: M. Ghebleh, S. Al-Yakoob, A. Kanso and D. Stevanovic, *Graphs having two main eigenvalues and arbitrarily many distinct vertex degrees*, *Applied Mathematics and Computation* **495** (2025), article **129311**, DOI [10.1016/j.amc.2025.129311](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amc.2025.129311), gives unboundedly many distinct degrees in harmonic / two-main-adjacency-eigenvalue graphs. Harmonic graphs satisfy s(v)=c d(v) and thus concern alpha=0, **not** s=-d^2/2+c d at fixed A_alpha parameter 1/3. Compare the earlier 2013 Deng--Huang theorem for Q parabolas, whose quadratic coefficient is -1, not -1/2. Neither is an exact predecessor of this construction. The 2025 article's publisher full text was inaccessible; its accessible detailed introduction and sections/abstract do not support an equivalence assertion. Claiming full novelty is unjustified without stronger coverage.

**NOT FOUND** as a published sequence, a complete list of isomorphism classes, or a previous enumeration of equality in Nikiforov's **(20)--(21)** for n=3,...,10. The figures and nauty/graph6 certificates are supplied by the lab, not independently established by this *priority* investigation. Closely neighboring work does not count as an earlier publication of the census: harmonic / integral harmonic graphs classify A d=c d at alpha=0 (e.g. M. Petrovic, B. Borovicanin, Z. Neither the ordinary graph census OEIS A001349 nor Graph Atlas class counts enumerate this **newly specified spectral property**. Lack of a sequence search hit is not a proof no one computed the sequence.
